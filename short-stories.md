Once upon a time, there was a girl named Ellia living in the woods. 
She lived in a small cottage in the middle of the forest. 
Beside her tiny cottage was a small pond where she did her laundry.
Ellia's life might be a bit peculiar compared to other girls living in the city.
But she doesn't mind; in fact, she likes it. She likes living in the woods.
No noisy people walking down the streets, no drunk people knocking on your door, mistaking your house for theirs at three in the morning.
She likes the quiet. She likes it very much.
